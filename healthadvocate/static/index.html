Before you record

Recording laws differ by place — some require only your own consent, others require everyone on the call to know. Doctors' offices and insurers may have their own policies. HealthAdvocate cannot advise you on the law; check yours before recording.

      Help & Real Humans

      HealthAdvocate is software, not a clinician, and some decisions belong to a person. These widely published US resources point to real people. HealthAdvocate never contacts anyone on your behalf.

      when software isn't enough, a person answers.

      Crisis support (US)

      988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text 988, any time.

      SAMHSA National Helpline — call 1-800-662-4357 (1-800-662-HELP; 24/7 treatment referral).

      How HealthAdvocate reports itself

      When a result could not be produced safely, you see a "This needs a human decision" notice instead of an answer. Urgency that reads "Model unavailable" means no urgency assessment was made — it is not a rating. Cross-checks between extraction methods are informal name overlaps, not accuracy checks. Analysis taken from recordings is labeled by where it came from — quoted from the transcript, inferred by a model, or unverified — and unverified items are always marked as needing your confirmation.

      Delete this recording?

      The recording and its transcript would be removed from this device. This cannot be undone. (Demo — nothing is really stored.)

      HealthAdvocate is here to support, not replace, professional medical guidance. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for diagnosis and treatment. Your information stays on your device.
